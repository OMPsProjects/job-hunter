## Rebuilt the credit memo template
Tags: process improvement, credit, teamwork, initiative
Situation: Credit memos at Example Lending took about three weeks from term sheet to committee, mostly rework.
Task: Cut the rework without loosening the credit standards.
Action: Mapped the questions committee asked most often, rebuilt the template around them, and walked each underwriter through it.
Result: Memos reached committee about a week sooner, and the team still uses the template.

## Walked away from a deal the sponsor wanted
Tags: judgment, disagreement, risk, communication
Situation: A bridge loan sponsor pushed for higher leverage on a property with falling occupancy.
Task: Decide whether the loan still fit our credit box and explain the call to the originator.
Action: Re-ran the cash flow on trailing numbers instead of the pro forma, and showed the originator where coverage broke.
Result: We passed. The originator brought the sponsor back with a lower ask, and that version closed.
